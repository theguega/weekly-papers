G$^3$VLA: Geometric inductive bias for Vision-Language-Action Models

Yue Peng, Yongzhe Zhao, Artur Habuda et al.  ·  2026-06-23

Injects calibrated 3D geometric inductive biases into pretrained VLAs using ray embeddings, projective positional encoding, and cross-view fusion.

ProblemPretrained VLAs process multi-camera views as independent 2D images, ignoring calibrated camera geometry and 3D spatial relationships.ApproachIntegrates intrinsic-conditioned ray embeddings, projective positional encoding (PRoPE), and bidirectional cross-view fusion into the visual stream. Supervision utilizes point maps or distilled predictions from a π³X teacher model.ResultYields consistent performance gains across LIBERO, RoboCasa24, and real-robot benchmarks, showing the strongest improvements on spatially sensitive tasks.Why it mattersEnables 3D spatial grounding in vision-heavy robot policies without altering the action space or requiring manual depth annotations.

Kejing Wang, Toan Nguyen, Minh Hoang Nguyen et al.  ·  2026-06-24

Robust online VLA adaptation using advantage-guided self-distillation to convert sparse rewards into dense action-token supervision.

ProblemSparse rewards provide weak supervision for high-dimensional autoregressive VLA policies, while text-based privileged teachers suffer from a modality gap.ApproachConstructs a proximal teacher in action space by perturbing action-token logits with calibrated advantage estimates. dVLA-RL: Reinforcement Learning over Denoising Trajectories for Discrete Diffusion Vision-Language-Action Models

Yuhao Wu, Yitian Liu, Weijie Shen et al.  ·  2026-06-22

Enables RL for discrete diffusion VLAs by optimizing the joint probability of denoising trajectories within an MDP framework.

ProblemStandard RL is inapplicable to discrete diffusion VLAs because the marginal probability of the final action is intractable. Consequently, these models have been limited to supervised learning from demonstration data.ApproachFormulates the denoising process as a Markov Decision Process to optimize the joint probability of the sampled generation path. A unified step scheduling mechanism varies denoising iterations based on task difficulty to balance performance and efficiency.ResultAchieved 99.7% success on LIBERO and a 30.6% improvement over SFT on RoboTwin 2.0. Performance matches or exceeds strong World-Action Model baselines.Why it mattersEstablishes a tractable RL objective for discrete diffusion policies, allowing VLA performance to exceed supervised baselines through environmental interaction. Essential for engineers scaling generalist manipulation policies.

ProblemEndoscopic navigation in ERCP is constrained by specular reflections, tissue occlusions, and the need for safe contact recovery. Drop-Then-Recovery: How Redundant Are Vision-Language-Action Models?

Guoheng Sun, Kaixi Feng, Shwai He et al.  ·  2026-06-26

Evaluates VLA redundancy via Drop-Then-Recovery, revealing significant language backbone over-parameterization compared to vision and action components.

ProblemVLA models inherit oversized language backbones that may exceed the capacity required for simple robotic instructions. The necessity of specific transformer blocks for closed-loop control remains poorly understood.ApproachThe Drop-Then-Recovery protocol uses GateProbe, a one-shot virtual-gate sensitivity metric, to rank and prune transformer blocks by action-loss contribution. Pruned models are subsequently fine-tuned to assess the recoverability of different architectural components.ResultRemoving 50% of LLM blocks improved OpenVLA-OFT success from 95.0% to 98.3% on LIBERO. Language backbones show high redundancy, whereas vision and action components are significantly less tolerant to removal.Why it mattersProvides a framework to reduce VLA inference latency and memory by pruning redundant LLM blocks. Indicates that current benchmarks under-utilize language capacity, suggesting a need for more balanced architectural allocation.

Improving Vision-Language-Action Model Fine-Tuning with Structured Stage and Keyframe Supervision

Yuan Xu, Yixiang Chen, Kai Wang et al.  ·  2026-06-25

Improves VLA fine-tuning using auxiliary stage classification and keyframe prediction heads derived automatically from gripper state transitions.

ProblemUniform action supervision ignores task-specific temporal structure, causing VLAs to fail at critical gripper-event transitions in long-horizon manipulation. This leads to poor representation of distinct manipulation stages during fine-tuning.ApproachStaKe adds lightweight auxiliary heads for stage classification and next-keyframe joint action prediction. These supervision signals are automatically extracted from demonstration gripper states, enriching internal representations without requiring manual annotation or changing the inference architecture.ResultAchieved relative success rate improvements of 14% in bimanual simulation and 56% in real-world Franka tasks, with peak performance gains in long-horizon sequences.Why it mattersProvides a plug-and-play auxiliary loss framework to stabilize VLA fine-tuning and improve transition-point accuracy without increasing inference-time computational overhead.

Learning Action Priors for Cross-embodiment Robot Manipulation

Dong Jing, Tianqi Zhang, Jiaqi Liu et al.  ·  2026-06-24

Pretrains a flow-matching action module on unconditioned trajectories to provide motion priors before vision-language-action alignment.

ProblemVLA models lack explicit motion priors, forcing inefficient simultaneous optimization of temporal dynamics and cross-modal alignment, especially in cross-embodiment settings.ApproachUses a two-stage framework: Stage 1 pretrains a flow-matching action encoder-decoder on raw trajectories. Stage 2 transfers this prior to VLA training via decoder reuse and latent distillation while using the encoder for compact history compression.ResultAcross 13 cross-embodiment tasks, the method demonstrates faster convergence, higher success rates, and improved performance on data-scarce real-world platforms.Why it mattersProvides a scalable strategy to leverage action-only data for better VLA initialization and efficient temporal history integration.
