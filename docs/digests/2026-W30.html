Jianguo Yu, Rukang Wang, Duanfeng Chu et al.  ·  2026-07-15

Decouples semantic and spatial streams in VLAs to prevent spatial representation collapse and improve trajectory planning accuracy.

ProblemStandard VLA models suffer from spatial representation collapse because discrete language tokens fail to capture continuous geometric priors. This entanglement prevents the model from maintaining the fine-grained spatial awareness required for precise, boundary-aware navigation.ApproachThe architecture separates a semantic stream for intent reasoning from a dedicated spatial stream that preserves uncompressed visual features. It employs hierarchical bridging for multi-scale feature extraction and a dual-stream planning adapter to fuse semantic intent with geometric constraints via cascaded attention.ResultEstablished a new state-of-the-art on the NAVSIM benchmark with a 87.1 Predictive Driver Model Score and a 98.4% No Collision rate. Why it mattersProvides a scalable method to overcome the 'language bottleneck' in VLAs, ensuring high-precision control without losing the reasoning capabilities of large vision-language models.

Action QFormer uses instruction-conditioned queries to isolate action-supervised representation shaping from core multimodal backbones.

DiMaS: Distribution Matching for Steering Vision-Language-Action Models

Pegah Khayatan, Sara Meziane, Jayneel Parekh et al.  ·  2026-07-15

Controls flow-matching VLA behavior by transporting representation distributions instead of applying linear vector shifts.

ProblemStandard linear representation steering fails in flow-matching VLAs because behavioral features are linearly decodable but not linearly steerable. This prevents effective post-hoc control over robotic execution styles.ApproachDiMaS implements distribution-matching to transport representation manifolds between target behaviors rather than using fixed vector offsets. It leverages optimal transport principles to align internal activation distributions of the action expert while preserving policy-critical structure.ResultEffectively steers behavior across multiple SOTA VLAs and generalizes to unseen tasks where linear steering fails. Analysis proves distribution-matching is required because action features occupy non-linear manifolds in the latent space.Why it mattersProvides a method for fine-grained behavioral control and safety intervention in VLA policies without expensive retraining. Essential for engineers tuning robot motion characteristics or enforcing operational constraints post-deployment.

ExToken: Structured Exploration for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Reinforcement Fine-tuning

Yilun Kong, Yunpeng Qing, Guozheng Ma et al.  ·  2026-07-14

Structured VLA exploration using discrete behavioral priors from offline demonstrations to improve reinforcement learning sample efficiency.

ProblemVLA reinforcement learning suffers from high interaction costs and exploration stagnation due to insufficient trajectory diversity during rollouts.ApproachExToken conditions the VLA policy on discrete behavioral tokens learned from offline data to enforce diverse exploration modes. A state-conditioned token selector is then trained to predict optimal behavioral modes for deterministic inference in unseen scenarios.ResultThe method significantly accelerates convergence and improves success rates across simulated and real-world manipulation tasks under restricted interaction budgets.Why it mattersProvides a practical framework for fine-tuning large-scale VLA models with RL by reducing the sample complexity of environment interactions.
