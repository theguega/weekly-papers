Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Robot Learning: A Dexterous Manipulation Study on Reactive Catching

Kejia Ren, Gaotian Wang, Andrew S. Morgan et al.  ·  2026-05-10

Introduces Domain-Randomized Instance Set (DRIS) to propagate multiple dynamics instances simultaneously for robust zero-shot sim-to-real transfer.

ProblemStandard domain randomization samples one dynamics instance per episode, insufficiently capturing real-world uncertainty in physics-intensive dexterous manipulation tasks.ApproachDRIS propagates a set of randomized physics instances concurrently during training. This forces the policy to output actions that are robust across multiple potential dynamical outcomes simultaneously.ResultEnabled successful zero-shot transfer for reactive catching on a flat plate using only ten concurrent instances, requiring no real-world fine-tuning.Why it mattersOffers a scalable technique for handling high-frequency, contact-rich manipulation tasks where perception noise and modeling errors typically break standard VLA policies.

PriorVLA: Prior-Preserving Adaptation for Vision-Language-Action Models

Xinyu Guo, Bin Xie, Wei Chai et al.  ·  2026-05-11

Preserves pretrained VLA priors using a frozen Prior Expert and query-based adaptation for robust, few-shot robot manipulation.

ProblemFull fine-tuning shifts broad VLA priors toward narrow downstream distributions, causing performance degradation in out-of-distribution and few-shot scenarios.ApproachMaintains a frozen Prior Expert while training an Adaptation Expert using only 25% of parameters. Expert Queries extract scene and motor priors from the frozen model to guide the specialized adaptation process.ResultSurpassed pi0.5 by 24 points in few-shot real-world tasks and achieved 99.1% success on LIBERO, showing significant OOD gains.Why it mattersProvides a parameter-efficient method to adapt VLAs without losing generalization, critical for scaling robot learning with limited downstream data.

Hand-in-the-Loop: Improving Dexterous VLA via Seamless Interventional Correction

Zhuohang Li, Liqun Huang, Wei Xu et al.  ·  2026-05-14

A seamless intervention method for dexterous VLA that eliminates gesture jumps and command mismatches during human-in-the-loop correction.

ProblemHigh-DoF dexterous manipulation suffers from "gesture jumps" during human intervention due to state-command mismatches between autonomous policies and teleoperation. This causes compounding errors and grasp failures in long-horizon contact-rich tasks.ApproachHandITL blends human corrective intent with autonomous policy execution to ensure smooth bimanual transitions. This method preserves motion continuity during takeover, preventing abrupt robot-hand configuration changes and maintaining stable grasps.ResultHandITL reduces takeover jitter by 99.8% and grasp failures by 87.5%, while refined policies achieve 19% higher success rates than teleoperation baselines.Why it mattersEssential for engineers using Interactive Imitation Learning to scale high-DoF dexterous manipulation without data corruption from discontinuous control transitions.

RotVLA: Rotational Latent Action for Vision-Language-Action Model

Qiwei Li, Xicheng Gong, Xinghang Li et al.  ·  2026-05-13

A VLA framework using continuous SO(n) rotational latent actions and flow-matching for structured, cross-embodiment robot learning.

ProblemDiscrete quantization in existing Latent Action Models causes trivial reconstruction behavior and lacks the geometric structure needed for heterogeneous datasets.ApproachModels latent actions as continuous elements of SO(n) using a triplet frame learning framework to capture temporal dynamics. A VLM backbone with a flow-matching head uses these latents as high-level plans to guide downstream robot action generation.ResultAchieved 98.2% success on LIBERO and 89.6% on RoboTwin2.0, outperforming existing models despite having only 1.7B parameters.Why it mattersProvides a geometrically grounded alternative to VQ-based latent actions, improving representation capacity for human-to-robot transfer and multi-embodiment pretraining.

LoopVLA: Learning Sufficiency in Recurrent Refinement for Vision-Language-Action Models

Boyang Shen, Kaixiang Yang, Hao Wang et al.  ·  2026-05-11

A recurrent VLA architecture that learns to dynamically stop refinement using self-supervised sufficiency scores for efficient robot control.

ProblemFixed-depth VLA backbones waste computation on simple tasks and often over-abstract low-level geometric cues essential for precise closed-loop manipulation.ApproachA shared Transformer block iteratively refines multimodal tokens, generating a candidate action and a sufficiency score at each step. These scores are trained via a self-supervised distribution alignment objective that links confidence to the relative quality of intermediate predictions.ResultReduces parameter count by 45% and improves inference throughput by 1.7x while matching or exceeding success rates on LIBERO and VLA-Arena.Why it mattersEnables dynamic inference-time compute allocation and high-frequency control by decoupling representation refinement from fixed model depth.

Yuanfang Peng, Jingjing Fu, Chuheng Zhang et al.  ·  2026-05-13

Enhances VLA robustness by combining PPO with auxiliary action invariance and sensitivity objectives over paired visual variants.

ProblemStandard RL rewards fail to distinguish between task-irrelevant visual shifts and behavior-altering state changes, leading to poor OOD deployment performance.ApproachAdds two auxiliary PPO objectives: an invariance term aligning action distributions for task-preserving variants and a sensitivity term separating distributions for task-altering variants. This utilizes paired visual augmentations to provide behavior-level guidance on which features to ignore or prioritize.ResultImproved success rates over standard PPO by 16.62% for pi-0.5 and 9.10% for OpenVLA across diverse OOD visual shifts in ManiSkill3.Why it mattersOffers a scalable method to fine-tune large VLA models for robustness against distractors, lighting, and viewpoint shifts without losing sensitivity to task-relevant object poses.

Shijie Lian, Bin Yu, Xiaopeng Lin et al.  ·  2026-05-14

History-conditioned intent encoding for VLA policies to resolve action ambiguity and ensure temporal consistency across action chunks.

ProblemFrame-conditioned policies often resample different intents at each step under partial observability, causing conflicting actions and unstable execution.ApproachEncodes recent visual history into a compact short-horizon intent representation to condition transformer-based action chunk generation. This ensures subsequent chunks align with established task phases.ResultAchieves superior performance and rollout stability across AliasBench, SimplerEnv, LIBERO, and RoboCasa compared to state-of-the-art VLA baselines.Why it mattersProvides a scalable method to handle multimodal demonstration data and observation aliasing, which are critical for robust real-world robot learning.
