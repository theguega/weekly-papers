Teaching Vision-Language-Action Models to Stop Safely

Zheyuan Liu, Chuhan Wang, Jinduo Guo et al.  ·  2026-11-19

State-Aware Fallback Imitation (SAFI) enables VLA models to safely stop prohibited tasks mid-execution while maintaining physical stability.

ProblemVLA policies often resume prohibited tasks when initialized mid-trajectory and cause physical incidents like dropping held objects during suppression.ApproachSAFI constructs fallback targets using stationary arm poses and consistent gripper states for prohibited demonstrations. It then performs joint policy adaptation to train the model on these fallback behaviors alongside standard task data.ResultSAFI reduced mid-execution task completion to 1.3% with zero physical incidents or action saturation while preserving performance on retained tasks.Why it mattersProvides a robust method for mid-trajectory safety intervention, ensuring robots stop safely without damaging hardware or losing control of grasped payloads.
