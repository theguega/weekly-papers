Improves VLA reliability by weighting critical planning actions using causal importance within GRPO advantage estimation.

ProblemOptimization methods treat routine execution and critical planning actions equally, leading to poor credit assignment for task-determining decisions. DEFLECT: Delay-Robust Execution via Flow-matching Likelihood-Estimated Counterfactual Tuning for VLA Policies

Yixiang Zhu, Yonghao Chen, Rui Meng et al.  ·  2026-05-19

Post-training refinement using flow-matching likelihood ratios to mitigate prediction-execution misalignment caused by asynchronous VLA inference latency.

ProblemAsynchronous inference causes misalignment between past observations and future execution states. This state drift leads to catastrophic performance drops as inference delay increases relative to the control frequency.ApproachConstructs label-free counterfactual pairs of fresh and stale actions from a reference policy to generate preference signals. Optimizes the policy via an implicit flow-matching likelihood-ratio surrogate to align stale predictions with future states.ResultYields a +6.4 success-rate gain in high-latency regimes and consistent improvements on real-robot reactive tasks like whack-a-mole.Why it mattersProvides a drop-in offline solution for deploying large, slow VLA models in high-frequency control loops without requiring online rollouts.
