SparkVLA: Stop-Aware Hierarchical VLA with Adaptive Action Chunking for Long-Horizon Manipulation

Xunyao Lei, Renjun Wu, Tianlin Huo et al.  ·  2026-08-17

A hierarchical VLA unifying subtask termination and adaptive action-prefix selection into a single ordinal ranking objective.

ProblemSubtask termination and action-prefix length are mutually dependent yet typically decoupled, causing synchronization errors in hierarchical VLA systems.ApproachFormulates termination and prefix length as a single ranking problem where Stop competes with every action-prefix candidate. Employs Anchor-Conditioned Context Encoding for task-relevant token pruning and a dedicated selection head for boundary scoring.ResultAchieved a 47.12% success rate on RoboCerebra, surpassing the official baseline by 30.57% and outperforming previous methods in real-world experiments.Why it mattersSolves critical synchronization issues in hierarchical robot control and eliminates the need for manual threshold tuning in long-horizon manipulation.

Just Noticeable Difference Modeling for Token Compression in Vision-Language-Action Models

Zhuoyuan Li, Rui Zhao, Jin Wang et al.  ·  2026-08-21

Action-JND compresses VLA tokens by predicting the maximum tolerable perturbation that preserves downstream robot action precision.

ProblemGeneric token compression metrics like attention or similarity ignore how feature changes impact action stability and safety in closed-loop control.ApproachIntroduces a lightweight estimator to predict per-token action-tolerance scores based on language-conditioned policy responses. These scores guide token pruning and stale-KV reuse by identifying tokens whose modification minimally deviates the final action output.ResultOn the LIBERO benchmark using OpenVLA, Action-JND consistently improves compression reliability and success rates, especially under aggressive compression ratios.Why it mattersProvides a safety-aware compression criterion for deploying heavy VLAs on latency-sensitive hardware without sacrificing robot manipulation accuracy.

LIBERO-VIFO: Benchmarking the Capability and Safety of Visual Cue Following in Vision-Language-Action Models

Zhengyan Qian, Rui Yan, Alex Jinpeng Wang et al.  ·  2026-08-18

A benchmark evaluating Vision-Language-Action models on their ability to follow authorized visual cues and ignore unauthorized ones.

ProblemVLA models lack systematic evaluation for diverse visual cue forms and the safety risks associated with following unauthorized or conflicting visual triggers.ApproachDefines eight visual cue families and four evaluation protocols focusing on authorized cue understanding, execution, and unauthorized following under language-cue conflict or empty instructions.ResultTesting seven VLAs reveals that cue understanding often fails to translate to action, while models dangerously execute tasks from unauthorized cues even without language instructions.Why it mattersEstablishes visual-centric safety as a critical research area, identifying vulnerabilities where robots might be manipulated through unintended or malicious visual environment triggers.

HAF: Adapting Generalist VLAs to Humanoid Whole-Body Loco-manipulation via Hierarchical Action Flow and Spectral Latent RL

Langzhe Gu, Chengkai Hou, Meng Li et al.  ·  2026-08-17

Adapts generalist VLAs to humanoid whole-body control using hierarchical flow-matching and spectral-compressed latent reinforcement learning.

ProblemHigh-dimensional humanoid loco-manipulation suffers from poor coordination in single-stage VLAs, while online refinement of large backbones is computationally prohibitive.ApproachHAF-VLA decomposes action denoising into sequential stages using cross-stage KV caches to maintain kinematic dependencies. HAF-Steer implements offline-to-online RL by optimizing a regularized SAC policy within a DCT-compressed noise subspace.ResultThe framework outperformed vanilla single-stage VLA baselines across seven real-world humanoid loco-manipulation tasks while enabling efficient policy refinement without backbone fine-tuning.Why it mattersProvides a blueprint for hierarchical whole-body control and computationally efficient online adaptation for large-scale flow-matching foundation models.

CertVLA: Certified Defense against Physical Visual Attacks for Vision-Language-Action Models

Hui Lu, Zhijie Peng, Yuqi Lin et al.  ·  2026-08-21

First certified defense for VLA policies against localized physical patch and texture attacks in continuous closed-loop control.

ProblemExisting certified defenses target discrete classification, failing to handle the continuous, high-dimensional, and temporally correlated action spaces of VLAs.ApproachUses deterministic covering masks to ensure an attack-free subset and certifies actions through behavioral consistency checks between mask pairs. It extends certificates to closed-loop rollouts by conjoining query-level decisions and calibrating episode scores for finite-sample clean coverage.ResultGuarantees that certified rollouts execute only actions consistent with attack-free predictions, with validated performance across real-world and simulation benchmarks.Why it mattersCrucial for safety-critical robotics, providing a mathematical guarantee against adversarial physical perturbations that is independent of specific attack methods or patch content.

ForeTime-VLA: Causal Future-Token Distillation from a World Action Model for Conveyor-Belt Manipulation

Siyuan Ma, Yutian Zhang, Boshi Zhang et al.  ·  2026-08-21

Distills future-aware latent representations from a frozen World Action Model into a causal VLA for dynamic robot manipulation.

ProblemStandard reactive VLAs lack the anticipation required for moving objects, while deploying full World Action Models is too slow. Engineers need predictive capabilities without the high inference cost of video-scale imagination.ApproachForeTime-VLA distills a frozen WAM teacher's future latents into a history-conditioned encoder predicting 64-D whitened targets. It augments VLM prefixes with future and phase tokens, optimizing via flow-matching, relational geometry, and time-to-transition objectives.ResultReduced test MAE by 2.63% and improved fast-belt grasp success from 6.7% to 36.7% over the pi0.5 baseline. Real-robot slow-moving grasp success reached 58.9%, outperforming the next-best reference by 22.2 percentage points.Why it mattersDemonstrates a practical distillation pipeline to embed temporal foresight into VLAs for high-speed tasks without the latency of generative models. Offers a scalable way to leverage world models for real-time robot control.

Systematic empirical study and unified framework identifying critical design choices for latent action models in robot manipulation.

EATR-Stereo: Embodiment-Aware Token Routing of Paired Stereo Evidence for Humanoid Vision-Language-Action Control

Songwei Wu, Rui Zhao, Fan Yang et al.  ·  2026-08-18

Embodiment-aware token routing integrates stereo views into frozen VLAs using primary-aligned auxiliary tokens and proprioceptive conditioning.

ProblemStandard VLA interfaces fail to integrate head-mounted stereo evidence without disrupting pretrained representations or handling viewpoint-specific occlusions during humanoid tasks.ApproachThe framework preserves primary-view tokens while generating primary-aligned Cross-View Auxiliary Tokens (CVATs) via cross-attention. A body-segmented proprioceptive encoder dynamically routes these auxiliary tokens based on robot configuration history.ResultAchieved 60.0% full-task success on a 33-DoF humanoid and improved recovery under asymmetric occlusion from 30% to 80% compared to non-routed baselines.Why it mattersProvides a scalable architecture to augment frozen VLAs with stereo-vision and embodiment awareness, essential for robust humanoid manipulation and spatial grounding.
