FM-VLA: Force-based Memory for Vision-Language-Action Models in Contact-Rich Manipulation

Ruicheng Li, Qixiu Li, Ruichun Ma et al.  ·  2026-07-20

Integrates force-history tokens into VLA models via VAEs to enable memory-dependent, contact-rich robotic manipulation.

ProblemStandard VLAs struggle with non-Markovian tasks where visual feedback is ambiguous or subtle, such as counting repeated contact-rich interactions.ApproachA VAE encodes force-sensor time series into compact latent tokens. These tokens, alongside state history, condition the action expert module to provide temporal context without the overhead of image-based memory.ResultAchieved over 80% success rate on memory-dependent tasks like repeated wiping and button pressing, significantly outperforming vision-only baselines.Why it mattersDemonstrates haptic-temporal conditioning as a lightweight, effective alternative to vision-based memory for complex, contact-driven robot manipulation.

Mengfei Zhao, Dihong Huang, Yikai Tang et al.  ·  2026-07-23

A community-driven data engine for scaling robot manipulation via browser-based teleoperation and automated task generation.

ProblemRobot data scaling is limited by specialized hardware requirements, centralized operator dependencies, and static, non-extensible task suites.ApproachUtilizes browser-based teleoperation for remote collection combined with automated task synthesis, success validation, and quality-filtering pipelines. It transforms raw demonstrations into training-ready trajectories through visual and physics-based augmentation and trajectory smoothing.ResultAggregated 50K+ trajectories across 207 tasks; continual pretraining improved VLA success rates by 5.8% and outperformed RoboCasa365-pretrained models by 37.3%.Why it mattersProvides a scalable framework for decentralized data collection and demonstrates clear performance gains in VLA robustness against sensor noise and environmental perturbations.

Asynchronous Multimodal Diffusion Policy Composition via Latency-Aware Guidance Fusion

Zihao He, Hongjie Fang, Shirun Tang et al.  ·  2026-07-19

Compositional diffusion policy framework using latency-aware guidance and action rebasing for asynchronous multimodal robot control.

ProblemMultimodal policies face performance bottlenecks when synchronizing sensors with heterogeneous latencies and sensing rates like slow vision and fast force feedback.ApproachDecouples modalities into independent experts providing asynchronous denoising guidance. It utilizes a reference-frame rebasing rule to align delayed relative action trajectories before fusion.ResultDemonstrates improved responsiveness and task success over synchronous fusion and specialized baselines in contact-rich manipulation tasks.Why it mattersProvides a scalable method to integrate high-latency VLA components with high-frequency tactile or force feedback without sacrificing control bandwidth.

Emergent Compositional Skills in Mixture-of-Experts VLAs

Shlok Shah, Rhiaan Jhaveri, Tharun Kumar Tiruppali Kalidoss et al.  ·  2026-07-22

Leverages Mixture-of-Experts action heads in VLAs to achieve emergent task decomposition and reusable behavioral primitives without manual supervision.

ProblemStandard end-to-end robot policies lack modularity and interpretability, hindering the discovery and reuse of fundamental skills across complex tasks.ApproachReplaces the monolithic VLA action head with a simplified Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) structure. The router learns temporal sequencing while experts specialize in low-level control primitives through end-to-end imitation learning.ResultMoE-VLAs match monolithic baseline performance while demonstrating significant expert reuse and qualitative specialization into distinct movement patterns across tasks.Why it mattersOffers a mechanism for automatic skill discovery and modularity, enabling more interpretable and potentially transferable robot control policies.
