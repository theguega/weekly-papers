Decomposes bimanual control using a view-selective visual router and interaction-aware Mixture-of-Experts for adaptive coordination.

ProblemMonolithic VLA models struggle with dynamic visual relevance across multiple cameras and shifting between independent and coordinated arm movements.ApproachA View-Selective Visual Router dynamically weights wrist-camera contributions while an Interaction-Aware Action Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) separates coordinated and arm-wise action generation pathways. This architecture provides explicit inductive biases for multi-view perception and bimanual task execution.ResultSuccess rates improved over monolithic baselines by 27.7% in RoboTwin 2.0 simulation and 43.3% in real-world long-horizon tasks.Why it mattersDemonstrates that structural decomposition of vision and action heads is superior to monolithic representations for complex bimanual robot manipulation.

ReCoVLA: VLM-Guided Reward Compilation for Failure Recovery in Vision-Language-Action Policies

Haodi Hu, Chung-Ta Huang, Jing Liu et al.  ·  2026-06-08

A framework using VLMs to compile structured rewards for training residual failure-recovery policies atop frozen VLA models.

ProblemPretrained VLA policies are brittle in off-nominal states and lack the targeted recovery behaviors needed to handle execution failures. Direct VLM action generation or reward estimation often lacks the precision required for complex, contact-rich manipulation recovery.ApproachAn external VLM identifies failure modes and stages to select specific reward components and masks from a structured library. This compiled reward trains a simulation-based residual policy that modulates the frozen VLA's output, followed by zero-shot sim-to-real deployment.ResultImproved average simulation success from 36.7% to 66.7% and achieved 61.7% success in zero-shot physical robot experiments, outperforming fine-tuned baselines.Why it mattersProvides a modular, data-efficient method to add robustness to any VLA without expensive fine-tuning or risking catastrophic forgetting of base priors.

Hao Shi, Weiye Li, Bin Xie et al.  ·  2026-06-08

Equips VLA models with a dual-stream memory bank and world-model imagination to solve long-horizon, temporally dependent robotic tasks.

ProblemStandard VLA models primarily rely on current observations, limiting their ability to handle long-horizon tasks that require historical context or future state prediction. Ambient Diffusion Policy: Imitation Learning from Suboptimal Data in Robotics

Adam Wei, Nicholas Pfaff, Thomas Cohn et al.  ·  2026-06-10

Selectively trains diffusion policies on suboptimal data by restricting contributions to specific noise levels based on spectral properties.

ProblemCo-training on suboptimal or out-of-distribution robot data often incorporates harmful artifacts into the policy. Scaling high-quality robot data is prohibitively expensive compared to using abundant but noisy demonstration sources.ApproachRestricts suboptimal data contribution to high and low diffusion time steps during training to exploit action-data spectral power laws. This method leverages the model's global-to-local hierarchy to extract useful structure while ignoring intermediate-frequency noise. It effectively filters harmful features through noise-dependent data usage.ResultOutperforms co-training baselines by up to 33% on Open X-Embodiment across diverse tasks and data qualities. Demonstrates robustness across noisy trajectories, sim-to-real gaps, and task-mismatch scenarios.Why it mattersProvides a scalable framework for training VLA models on massive, unstructured datasets like Open X-Embodiment without performance degradation. Allows engineers to leverage diverse, non-expert data sources for improved generalization.

Bridging the Morphology Gap: Adapting VLA Models to Dexterous Manipulation via Intent-Conditioned Fine-Tuning

Chuanke Pang, Junyi Huang, Zhijun Zhao et al.  ·  2026-06-10

Adapts low-DoF VLA models to dexterous manipulation using a two-stage decoupled architecture conditioned on virtual grasp intent.

ProblemPre-trained VLA models are limited to parallel grippers, causing catastrophic forgetting and action manifold collapse when fine-tuned for high-DoF hands.ApproachDecouples spatial reasoning from finger articulation by repurposing 1-DoF grasp outputs as a virtual intent proxy. Qingzi Wang, Xiyang Wu, Guangyao Shi et al.  ·  2026-06-09

Aligns VLA internal social representations with action heads via counterfactual and temporal post-training for safer robot navigation.

ProblemStandard behavior cloning fails to utilize a VLA's latent knowledge of pedestrians and collision risks, leading to unsafe navigation shortcuts.ApproachSALSA employs a two-stage post-training framework: social behavioral alignment using counterfactual human-object pairs and temporal safety alignment using automated future-risk supervision. It bridges intermediate-layer social features directly to the action head to ensure latent representations drive control output.ResultReduced near-collisions by 86.4% and increased social counterfactual accuracy from 53% to 93% in SCAND and real-world evaluations.Why it mattersDemonstrates that safety-critical social behaviors are already latent in pre-trained VLAs and can be unlocked through representation alignment without manual annotation.

Fan Zhang, Seongbin Park, Baharan Mirzasoleiman et al.  ·  2026-06-08

Training-free framework using hidden-state probes and Control Barrier Functions for autonomous failure recovery in VLA policies.

ProblemPre-trained VLA models lack robustness to environmental perturbations, frequently leading to unrecoverable grasping and placement failures.ApproachUses lightweight hidden-state probes to extract 3D object coordinates and a kinematic state machine for failure detection.
