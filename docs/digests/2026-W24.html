Integrating structured 2D/3D affordance forecasting as an intermediate representation within a Mixture-of-Transformer VLA framework for precise robotic manipulation.

ProblemStructural mismatches between high-level VLM semantic spaces and low-level embodied control policies lead to imprecise perception-action mappings and poor spatial grounding.ApproachUses a Mixture-of-Transformer architecture to model affordance through Which2Act (object grounding), Where2Act (2D localization), and How2Act (3D geometric reasoning). Jiaxin Shi, Xidong Zhang, Fucai Zhu et al.  ·  2026-06-03

Injects latent 3D geometry and spatial reasoning into 2D VLAs using hierarchical co-training and implicit reasoning anchor tokens.

ProblemStandard 2D VLAs lack inherent 3D spatial awareness and geometric priors, often relying on visual shortcuts that fail in complex manipulation tasks.ApproachAligns intermediate visual features with 3D foundation models and distills high-level spatial reasoning into a shared latent anchor token. Yitong Chen, Shiduo Zhang, Jingjing Gong et al.  ·  2026-06-04

Achieves high-performance one-step VLA action generation by biasing diffusion training noise distributions toward high-noise states without distillation.

ProblemIterative denoising in diffusion VLAs creates high inference latency. Standard one-step acceleration methods are optimized for high-dimensional images and are unnecessarily complex for low-dimensional robot action spaces.ApproachUses standard velocity prediction while biasing the training time distribution toward high-noise states. This encourages the model to recover the full action signal from Gaussian noise in a single step without auxiliary objectives.ResultOne-step policies matched or exceeded 10-step performance, achieving 95.6% success on LIBERO-Long using a 1.4B parameter VLM.Why it mattersProvides a simplified path to real-time, low-latency robot control. Eliminates the need for complex distillation or consistency model pipelines while maintaining diffusion-level expressiveness.

Jinhao Wu, Shiduo Zhang, Yicheng Liu et al.  ·  2026-06-05

Integrates coarse action-token trajectory planning into the VLA action head to improve long-horizon task execution.

Jointly optimizes Latent Action Models and VLAs through representation alignment to improve action grounding from unlabeled videos.

ProblemVLA models are limited by robot data scarcity, while decoupled training with Latent Action Models causes ungrounded representations and hallucinations.ApproachLARA aligns VLA action outputs with LAM latent representations during joint optimization. MPCoT: Reward-Guided Multi-Path Latent Reasoning for Test-Time Scalable Vision-Language-Action

Boyang Zhang, Lianlei Shan  ·  2026-06-04

Reward-guided latent multi-path reasoning for VLA that scales inference-time deliberation without generating extra text tokens.

ProblemVLA models struggle with long-horizon tasks due to limited one-pass deliberation and the high latency of text-based chain-of-thought.ApproachInitializes M latent hypotheses refined over K steps, then aggregates paths using a reward-guided scorer trained on expert consistency and world-model feedback. The architecture preserves original action token counts while enabling configurable depth-width scaling during inference.ResultDemonstrated improved long-horizon success rates on LIBERO and CALVIN benchmarks, showing that latent refinement significantly outperforms standard single-pass action decoding.Why it mattersEnables test-time scaling for robot policies, allowing engineers to trade compute for robustness without the latency overhead of autoregressive reasoning tokens.
