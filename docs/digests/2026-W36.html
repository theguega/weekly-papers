PHR-VLA: Planning Horizon Reasoning for Vision-Language-Action Models

Davood Soleymanzadeh, Kaidi Zhang, Zhiyuan Zhang et al.  ·  2026-08-27

Enhances VLAs with planning-horizon reasoning by aligning internal features with latent future dynamics using an auxiliary training head.

ProblemStandard VLAs lack explicit mechanisms to reason about future task dynamics. This deficiency hinders performance in contact-rich and fine-grained manipulation tasks that require anticipation.ApproachAn auxiliary head aligns the VLA's latent representations with future-state embeddings during training using privileged observations. It specifically utilizes patch-level dynamics from wrist-mounted and third-person cameras to supervise the internal feature space.ResultImproved LIBERO success rates to 88.4% and real-world disassembly tasks from 63.3% to 82.5% using local wrist-camera supervision.Why it mattersEnables predictive world-modeling capabilities within VLA policies, significantly boosting success in contact-rich tasks without increasing inference-time computational costs.

TrapVLA: Trapping Vision-Language-Action Models in Configured Failure Modes

Jun-Hui Liu, Kun-Yu Lin, Yi-Lin Wei et al.  ·  2026-08-27

Introduces a backdoor attack inducing specific robot failure modes via stealthy textual triggers and learned action residuals.

ProblemTraditional backdoor attacks lack control over specific failure outcomes. Existing methods cannot reliably induce precise, stealthy deviations like exact positional offsets while maintaining high clean-task performance.ApproachTrapVLA synthesizes target trajectories for specific failure modes and explicitly learns trigger-induced action residuals. This allows the model to steer policy behavior toward configured failure modes by modeling the delta from clean actions.ResultEffectively injected four failure modes across Trap-LIBERO and Trap-RoboTwin benchmarks with high fidelity. Real-world tests confirmed successful hijacking of VLA actions while preserving performance on clean data.Why it mattersExposes critical security vulnerabilities where textual triggers cause precise physical failures. Crucial for engineers building safety-critical robot policies using third-party foundation models or datasets.

MA-VLA: Multi-Arm Vision-Language-Action Model for Collaboration and Compositional Generalization

Zaibin Zhang, Junlan Xiao, Zhongbo Zhang et al.  ·  2026-08-26

A VLA framework using per-arm atomic action assignment and role-agnostic training for multi-arm compositional generalization.

ProblemCurrent VLAs rely on global instructions and lack mechanisms for assigning arm-specific behaviors. This prevents generalization to multi-arm coordination patterns not explicitly seen during training.ApproachDecomposes cooperative tasks into mid-level atomic prompts assigned to individual arms. Uses Arm Shuffle, a training-time permutation of observations and states, to enforce role-agnostic instruction following and enable zero-shot recomposition.ResultMA-VLA consistently succeeds on simulation and real-world benchmarks where test-time collaboration patterns were absent in training. Standard global-instruction VLAs fail to generalize to these unseen coordination requirements.Why it mattersProvides a structured method to scale multi-robot systems by decoupling coordination from execution. It enables flexible task allocation and improves robustness in complex collaborative manipulation environments.

GaussVLA: Geometry-Aware Spatial Reasoning for Vision-Language-Action Model

Md Selim Sarowar, Md Tanvir Islam, Sungho Kim et al.  ·  2026-08-25

Integrates 3D Gaussian spatial tokenization and depth-aware Chain-of-Thought reasoning into a parameter-efficient Mamba-based VLA architecture.

ProblemCurrent VLAs use flat 2D tokens or scalar depth values that lack intrinsic 3D structure, surface orientation, and geometric confidence for precise spatial reasoning.ApproachLifts 2D features into compact 3D Gaussian tokens using a Gaussian Spatial Tokenizer with learned queries. Implements a Depth-Aware Chain-of-Thought module for non-autoregressive geometric reasoning conditioned on language and flow-time.ResultAchieved 93.5% average success on LIBERO and 100% on the Spatial suite with only 200M parameters, outperforming SpatialVLA by 19.7% relatively.Why it mattersProvides a scalable method to inject explicit 3D priors into VLAs, enabling high-performance spatial manipulation without the overhead of massive transformer backbones.

Pointing-VLA: Typed Spatial Grounding Interfaces for Vision-Language-Action Manipulation

Xiwen Chen, Zelin Li, Zhiruo Zhou et al.  ·  2026-08-24

Typed spatial readout heads for VLA models providing non-textual geometric grounding for robot manipulation.

ProblemExisting VLA models rely on brittle autoregressive text coordinates or opaque action tokens, decoupling reasoning from precise execution.ApproachPointing-VLA replaces text-serialized geometry with geometry-specific heads predicting normalized points, object-functional grounding heatmaps, and trajectories. An explicit execution contract maps these outputs to PICK/PLACE targets for motion generation via CuRobo.ResultAchieved 72.9% success on Bridge/WidowX and improved real-robot success from 52.7% to 80.7% while decoding ~6.8x faster than text-based VLAs.Why it mattersProvides an efficient, inspectable, and high-performance interface for bridging VLA reasoning with low-level robot controllers and motion planners.

PredVLA: A Sub-Million-Parameter Predictive-Coding Policy for Robot Manipulation

Hiroki Sawada, Shunichi Kasahara  ·  2026-08-27

A 0.68M parameter predictive-coding policy using sensory prediction errors for language-conditioned robot manipulation and state correction.

ProblemLarge VLA models require massive scale and pretraining, questioning if smaller architectures can sustain competitive performance. Efficient models often lack explicit, measurable mechanisms for online state correction from observation errors.ApproachUses hierarchical generative recurrent dynamics to predict visual features and proprioception. Latent state updates occur exclusively via online inference from sensory prediction errors, enabling a purely predictive coding-based control loop.ResultAchieved 86.9% success on LIBERO short-horizon tasks, outperforming parameter-matched Transformer and LSTM baselines by 3.7x and 7.4x. Maintained 75.4% overall success with only 0.68 million parameters and no robot pretraining.Why it mattersProves that predictive-coding inductive biases allow sub-million-parameter models to rival larger VLAs. Offers a mathematically grounded method for isolating the contribution of closed-loop feedback in robot policies.

Structured sub-skill retrieval and hybrid reranking for data-efficient fine-tuning of Vision-Language-Action models on new tasks.

Beyond Data Scaling: Representation-Centric Continued Pre-training for Vision-Language-Action Models

Senqiao Yang, Chengyao Wang, Yuxin Chen et al.  ·  2026-08-27

Improves VLA generalization via representation-centric continued pre-training on multi-embodiment data while preserving vision-language priors.

ProblemRobot trajectories are harder to scale than web-scale data, causing models to overfit specific actions instead of learning transferable knowledge. Representation quality becomes the bottleneck under fixed robot-data budgets.ApproachVLAct employs continued pre-training on heterogeneous robot data using VLM-prior preservation and multi-head continuous action co-supervision. It utilizes a partially unified cross-embodiment action layout to bridge diverse morphologies while allowing task-specific fine-tuning.ResultAchieved 82.6% on LIBERO-Plus and 92.5% on RoboTwin 2.0, outperforming industrial baselines like ABot-M0. On unseen humanoid hardware, VLAct surpassed the full-data GR00T-N1.6 baseline using only 20% of downstream trajectories.Why it mattersProvides a compute-efficient strategy for cross-embodiment transfer, enabling high-performance VLA models on a modest 16-GPU budget. Validates that representation quality is an independent axis of progress alongside data scaling.

FlashVLA: Streaming Action Decoding for Fast and Asynchronous VLA Inference

Zekai Li, Jiaming Tang, Zhijian Liu  ·  2026-08-27

Streaming action decoding framework for flow-matching VLAs enabling high-frequency, temporally consistent asynchronous robot control.

ProblemHigh inference latency in flow-matching VLAs and temporal inconsistencies during asynchronous execution lead to unstable robotic control.ApproachFlashVLA utilizes a streaming buffer of action chunks at varying noise levels decoded via chunk-wise causal attention. This autoregressive formulation outputs one executable chunk per inference step while implicitly maintaining action continuity.ResultAchieves over 30Hz control frequency on a single GPU with smooth asynchronous execution and strong manipulation performance.Why it mattersProvides a deployment-ready solution for high-speed VLA inference, crucial for reactive robot behaviors and overcoming diffusion-based latency bottlenecks.
