Mag-VLA: Vision-Language-Action Model for Bimanual Magnetically Actuated Microrobot Manipulation

Yongchen Wang, Kangyi Lu, Lan Wei et al.  ·  2026-05-27

A hierarchical VLA framework for bimanual magnetic microrobot manipulation using a Qwen2.5-VL backbone and phase-conditioned action chunking.

ProblemControlling bimanual magnetically actuated microrobots is difficult due to nonlinear magnetic interactions and coupled control requirements in shared workspaces.ApproachAdapts a Qwen2.5-VL-7B backbone using LoRA for visual-language perception integrated with a motion-aware phase classifier. Generates coordinated trajectories using a phase-conditioned Action Chunking Transformer (ACT) decoder for temporal coherence.ResultMag-VLA achieved a 90% approach success rate and 50-80% transport success rates, significantly outperforming alternative generative action heads.Why it mattersProvides a blueprint for applying VLAs to non-contact, bimanual manipulation and non-linear physical regimes through hierarchical, phase-aware control.

DeMaVLA: A Vision-Language-Action Foundation Model for Generalizable Deformable Manipulation

Taiyi Su, Jian Zhu, Tianjian Wang et al.  ·  2026-05-29

A VLA model using layer-pruned action experts and flow matching for generalizable, multi-category deformable object manipulation.

ProblemGeneralizing deformable object manipulation is difficult due to high-dimensional state spaces and task interference during multi-category training. Current systems often rely on inefficient category-specific policies that fail to handle diverse materials and geometries.ApproachThe model uses a VLM backbone with a layer-pruned action expert to maintain alignment while reducing costs, employing flow matching for action generation. It undergoes large-scale pre-training on 5,000 dual-arm hours followed by human-in-the-loop DAgger to incorporate corrective trajectories.ResultDeMaVLA achieves competitive results on the RoboTwin benchmark and demonstrates strong zero-shot generalization in real-world household folding across various clothing categories.Why it mattersDemonstrates efficient VLA architecture design through pruning and highlights the necessity of corrective data aggregation for complex, contact-rich robot learning tasks.

ElegantVLA: Learning When to Think for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Models

Ye Li, Huanan Liu, Kangye Ji et al.  ·  2026-05-28

A phase-adaptive inference scheduler that dynamically reuses computation in VLA models to optimize real-time robot control frequency.

ProblemVLA models suffer from high inference latency because large backbones and iterative diffusion heads run redundantly at every step. This overlooks the non-uniform reasoning demands across sequential robotic manipulation phases.ApproachA lightweight scheduler evaluates temporal similarity and motion cues to dynamically allocate computation across the Vision-LLM and action head. It employs five levels of perception-language reuse and three levels of denoising state reuse without retraining the base model.ResultAchieved up to 3.77x speedup on CogACT and increased real-world control frequency from 13.8 Hz to 26.3 Hz on GR00T tasks.Why it mattersEnables high-frequency, real-time control for large VLA models by treating inference as a dynamic scheduling problem rather than a static computation.

ProgVLA: Progress-Aware Robot Manipulation Skill Learning

Seungsu Kim, Jinyoung Choi, Seungmin Baek et al.  ·  2026-05-27

A 0.1B-parameter VLA model using multi-modal resampling and auxiliary progress estimation for efficient long-horizon robot manipulation.

ProblemVLA models face significant compute constraints and struggle with tracking task progress over long-horizon sequences. Large-scale models often fail to maintain temporal grounding while remaining deployable on edge hardware.ApproachA two-stage Perceiver resampler compresses vision, language, and proprioception into fixed-length context tokens. Auxiliary progress heads are trained with offline RL to estimate remaining-horizon targets, guiding advantage-weighted flow-matching imitation learning.ResultThe 0.1B-parameter model exceeds larger pretrained baselines in success rates on long-horizon and multi-object tasks. Ablations confirm that context resampling and progress-aware training are the primary drivers of performance gains.Why it mattersProvides a method for achieving high-performance robot control with minimal parameters through explicit temporal reasoning. Engineers can use these techniques to deploy VLAs on compute-limited hardware without sacrificing long-horizon reliability.

FineVLA: Fine-Grained Instruction Alignment for Steerable Vision-Language-Action Policies

Xintong Hu, Xuhong Huang, Jinyu Zhang et al.  ·  2026-05-26

A framework for fine-grained action-aligned supervision enabling steerable VLA policies through detailed execution-level instructions.

ProblemStandard VLA datasets use coarse goal-level labels, missing critical execution details like approach direction, contact points, and arm selection. This lack of granularity limits the model's ability to follow precise procedural constraints or achieve high-precision manipulation.ApproachIntroduces FineVLA-Data, a dataset of 47k trajectories with fine-grained labels generated by a specialized VLM annotator. Develops a steerable policy trained on mixtures of fine-grained and raw goal-level instructions to balance high-level goals with low-level execution details.ResultOptimal instruction mixing yields a 62.7% real-world success rate, outperforming goal-only baselines by 12.8 points. Gains are most pronounced in steering-heavy tasks involving pose (+23) and approach direction (+18).Why it mattersProvides a scalable pipeline for densifying sparse robotic instruction signals via VLM-automated annotation. Essential for developing VLAs that require high-precision control and adherence to specific execution constraints.
