RL$^2$-VLA: Adaptive RL Latent Compositional Steering with Test-Time Scaling for Vision-Language-Action Models

Derek Ming Siang Tan, Shailesh Shailesh, Srikrishna Iyer et al.  ·  2026-07-29

Adaptive latent steering for VLAs using offline RL to improve out-of-distribution performance through selective inference-time intervention.

ProblemVLA models suffer from low action diversity and correlated failure modes in out-of-distribution tasks. Existing steering methods apply interventions indiscriminately, often perturbing successful trajectories.ApproachTrains a lightweight offline RL policy on VLA action latents to compose flow velocities with the frozen base model. Compositional steering is adaptively activated only when a failure predictor indicates the base policy is likely to fail.ResultAchieved up to a 17.3% increase in success rates across SIMPLER and PolaRiS benchmarks in out-of-domain settings.Why it mattersEnables modular, test-time performance scaling for large VLAs without costly retraining. It provides a principled way to inject RL-driven diversity into frozen imitation-learned policies.

WCM: A World Critic Model for Vision-Language-Action Reinforcement Learning

Senyu Fei, Xiaopeng Yu, Siyin Wang et al.  ·  2026-07-31

A joint world-modeling and value-estimation critic using LeJEPA to capture temporal dynamics for VLA reinforcement learning.

ProblemStandard VLA critics use single-frame observations, failing under partial observability. Scalar-return regression lacks sufficient supervision to learn temporal structure in high-dimensional visual spaces.ApproachWCM utilizes a lightweight LeJEPA architecture to jointly predict future latent states and estimate values. This explicitly trains the critic’s representation to capture temporal dynamics instead of relying solely on return regression.ResultAchieved state-of-the-art performance across 149 tasks, showing strong out-of-distribution generalization. Real-world tests on seven manipulation tasks confirmed stable deployment with Pi0.5 and OpenVLA-OFT backbones.Why it mattersProvides a robust framework for RL post-training of large VLAs, addressing core credit assignment and state representation issues in robotic control.

Zonghe Liu, Shanyuan Jie, Xiaoquan Sun et al.  ·  2026-07-28

Aligns VLA visual features with SAM3D-extracted 3D priors during training to improve spatial reasoning during RGB-only inference.

ProblemStandard VLAs lack fine-grained 3D understanding, struggling with object occlusion, pose variation, and precise spatial interactions.ApproachUses SAM3D as a frozen teacher to extract dense object-centric 3D representations from task-relevant masks. These representations are distilled into intermediate visual features of the pi_0 policy during training, preserving an RGB-only pipeline for inference.ResultAchieved a 99.1% success rate on LIBERO and a 4.11 average sequence length on CALVIN, demonstrating superior robustness in long-horizon manipulation.Why it mattersProvides a training-time-only strategy to imbue VLAs with 3D geometry awareness without requiring depth sensors or point cloud processing during deployment.

Ning Cheng, Jinan Xu, Wanlin Li et al.  ·  2026-07-27

Integrates spatiotemporal tactile representations into VLAs using JEPA-inspired future visual supervision for contact-rich robot manipulation.

ProblemHigh-dimensional tactile signals are difficult to integrate into pretrained VLAs due to data scarcity and complex temporal dynamics. Existing methods often rely on instantaneous contact states or low-dimensional wrench sequences.ApproachLearns action-conditioned tactile representations through future visual supervision in latent space using a JEPA-inspired framework. FA-RDP: A Frequency-Adaptive Reactive Diffusion Policy for Contact-Rich Manipulation

Lifeng Zhuo, Wendi Chen, Han Xue et al.  ·  2026-07-30

Dynamically switches between multi-step low-frequency and one-step high-frequency diffusion sampling for reactive, multimodal contact-rich manipulation.

ProblemFixed-frequency diffusion policies cannot simultaneously preserve pre-contact action multimodality and provide the high-frequency reactivity required for contact-rich force feedback.ApproachUses a shared multi-frequency Transformer and a learned multimodality indicator to switch between multi-step and one-step sampling. Manifold Consistency Distillation reparameterizes the network for manifold-constrained action prediction.ResultAchieved the highest success rates across three contact-rich tasks compared to fixed-frequency baselines while maintaining distinct pre-contact trajectory modes.Why it mattersProvides a framework for scaling diffusion policies to tasks requiring both complex long-horizon planning and millisecond-level reactive control.
