BICPO-VLA: Behavior-Identified Continuation Preference Optimization for Smooth Asynchronous Vision-Language-Action Control

Ming Shang, Yuchen Huang, Jiaoyang Chen et al.  ·  2026-08-14

Optimizes asynchronous VLA control using Haar-based action decomposition and preference alignment to bridge the request-to-handoff state gap.

ProblemAddressing the request-to-handoff gap in asynchronous control caused by intent ambiguity, inference-time state drift, and execution-time discontinuities.ApproachEmploys a causal history encoder for behavior identification and Haar subspace generation to accelerate inference. Uses BICPO with Flow-DPO to align generated action chunks with the actual future robot state at handoff.ResultReduces inference-induced latency and improves trajectory continuity by adapting action chunks to actual handoff states without altering intended behavior.Why it mattersProvides a framework for high-frequency, low-latency deployment of large VLA models by mitigating state drift and ensuring smooth trajectory continuation.

FIRE-VLA: Failure-Informed Self-Evolution for Vision-Language-Action Models in Autonomous Driving

Hao Dou  ·  2026-08-13

Improves driving VLA models via privileged self-distillation when standard relative policy optimization fails to provide useful signals.

ProblemRelative policy optimization like GRPO lacks a meaningful gradient signal when all sampled trajectories in a rollout group are failures.ApproachTriggers self-distillation on low-reward rollout groups using a teacher model with privileged access to the hidden future trajectory. This teacher supervises the student's action tokens while standard GRPO remains active for all groups.ResultReduced mean L2 planning error from 1.848m to 1.500m and decreased persistent failure prevalence from 13.03% to 11.20% on nuScenes.Why it mattersProvides a mechanism for robot-learning models to escape local minima in RL by using temporal future information as an internal supervision signal.

Neural Introspection Gating for Adaptive KV-Cache Reuse in Vision-Language-Action Models

Zhijie Wu, Kento Kawaharazuka, Kei Okada  ·  2026-08-11

A training-free gating mechanism for VLA KV-cache reuse using logit margins to trigger recomputation during low-confidence states.

ProblemExisting VLA KV-cache reuse methods rely on observation-space heuristics that ignore model uncertainty, causing accuracy drops in dynamic tasks.ApproachGated VLA-Cache monitors the logit margin between the top two action tokens as a zero-cost confidence signal. It triggers full visual recomputation whenever this margin falls below a threshold, overriding the similarity-based cache reuse.ResultOn LIBERO-Goal and LIBERO-Long, it recovered 100% of accuracy lost by blind caching while maintaining 80% of compute savings.Why it mattersProvides a robust way to optimize VLA inference latency for real-time control without sacrificing performance in high-uncertainty scenarios.

ProblemLarge VLAs waste capacity on task-irrelevant semantics, while pixel-level world models incur high costs by predicting unnecessary visual details. Temporal GRPO: Beyond Trajectory-Level Credit in Vision-Language-Action Reinforcement Learning

Yao Zhou, Hang Gao, Fengge Wu et al.  ·  2026-08-13

Decomposes GRPO advantages into stage-specific credits to prevent trajectory-level credit aliasing in VLA reinforcement learning.

ProblemStandard GRPO applies a single rollout-level reward to all actions, penalizing successful early-stage progress if a later stage fails. This credit aliasing hinders learning efficiency in long-horizon robotic tasks with sparse feedback.ApproachThe method segments trajectories into detectable task stages and computes relative advantages only between rollouts reaching the same stage. These localized advantages are then mapped to corresponding action intervals for a single policy update.ResultDemonstrates improved task success and sample efficiency on RoboTwin 2.0 and LIBERO-Long by isolating credit to specific points of failure.Why it mattersEnables more effective RL post-training for VLAs on multi-step manipulation tasks where global trajectory-level rewards are too coarse for fine-grained credit assignment.

XCoT-VLA: Executable Chain-of-Thought for Vision-Language-Action Driving

Foundation Model Team, XPeng Inc  ·  2026-08-11

Replaces verbose CoT with compact executable tokens for efficient, reasoning-conditioned trajectory generation in autonomous driving VLA models.

ProblemNatural-language Chain-of-Thought is too computationally expensive for real-time control and lacks an efficient optimization path for action-facing representations. Its open-ended nature makes it difficult to ground high-level reasoning into precise trajectories.ApproachIntegrates 2-6 compact executable tokens that condition flow-matching trajectory generation through shared multimodal self-attention and deterministic FFN routing. These tokens are learned from Reason-Action supervision and can be further refined using XCoT Policy Optimization (XCPO).ResultReduced longitudinal ADE from 1.645 to 1.323 and lateral lane-change FDE from 1.616 to 0.648, while maintaining real-time planning speeds.Why it mattersProvides a framework for integrating reasoning into VLA models without the latency penalties of natural language decoding, crucial for high-frequency robotic control.

Lost in Reconstruction: Aligning Action Representations with Language in Vision-Language-Action Models

Li Wenjie, Yash Jangir, Ignacy Stepka et al.  ·  2026-08-11

SALT aligns action tokenization with instructions using an auxiliary VLM objective to improve language-conditioned robot policy performance.

ProblemStandard VLA action tokenization relies on reconstruction losses that ignore linguistically meaningful motion nuances, leading to semantic information loss.ApproachSALT augments a VQ-VAE with an auxiliary objective forcing a frozen VLM to recover task instructions from quantized action latents. This ensures discrete action codes capture both execution fidelity and the semantic intent of the verb.ResultSALT achieved 71.9% success in SimplerEnv, outperforming reconstruction-only VQ-VAE at 42.7% and FAST at 31.2% while maintaining high trajectory reconstruction quality.Why it mattersProvides a method to bridge the gap between low-level robot control and high-level language by embedding semantic structure directly into the action representation.
